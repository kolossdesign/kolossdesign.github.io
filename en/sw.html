Three key products

The ecosystem rests on three products: the store for customers, the back office for Business Partners and the design system that ties them together. I owned each of them long term, across several generations of the interface.

The online store for the company’s customers. Four generations of the product in ten years: from the first version to a full product approach.

3. The design system as a product

Not a library of mockups but a product with its own users – designers and engineers – and its own development cycle.

Using a product approach we built a tokenised design system that unified every digital product of the company around shared components, tokens and design principles.

How my scope grew

  • 2015 – first product tasks
  • 2016 – the full eCommerce cycle
  • 2018 – eCommerce + back office + design system
  • 2021 – several product areas
  • 2023 – growing several key product areas

What this experience taught me

Strong products are never built in a single release. They only get better through consistent, incremental work that lets them grow together with the business and its users.
