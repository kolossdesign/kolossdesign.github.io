Grew turnover by 33% in a year by designing new trading tools

Role: Product Designer

Discovery • UX research • UX/UI • Design system • Delivery

An international crypto platform: exchange, wallet and payment services. The project is under an NDA, so it goes without a name and with anonymised screens.

Project results

+33%platform turnover in a year
3new trading tools
2new sections

What made this project different

  • The cost of a mistake is measured in the user’s money, not in inconvenience
  • A dense interface: a lot of data on one screen, every pixel taken
  • Two audiences in one product – beginners and entrepreneurs
  • Turnover growth was the direct measure of design success

The task

Increase the inflow of capital to the platform. Three specific tasks grew out of that: fill the platform with tools that help people trade; motivate trading through Rewards; handle the growing load in the trading section – which is why it had to be redesigned.

My scope was the whole platform: exchange, wallet and payment services.

What I did

Five independent areas, from virtual cards to trading alerts. Each one below.

1. Virtual cards

A section for issuing and managing virtual cards, so crypto can be spent like ordinary money. I designed the issuing flow, the limits and the transaction history.

2. The Rewards section

A rewards section with tasks, progress and payouts. An entertaining scenario inside a financial product is a separate challenge of tone and visual language: excitement must not undermine trust in the platform.

3. Redesign of the trading section

A high-load section: chart, order book, depth, trade history and the order form on one screen. I rebuilt the hierarchy and density so that a professional keeps their speed and a newcomer does not get lost.

4. Grid bots

Trading robots that place deals automatically according to configured algorithms. I designed the setup flow so that the user understands the grid logic before launching and sees what they are risking.

5. Trading alerts

Notifications about changes in an asset’s price. The task was to give a fast way to set a condition without turning the app into a stream of noise.

What this experience taught me

In a high-load interface the designer’s main job is not to hide complexity but to make every available tool visible at a glance. The user has to see the whole set of options at once: whatever stays out of sight does not exist for them, however convenient it may be.
